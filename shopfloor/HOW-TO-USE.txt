FLOORLINE — how to use this PC

You do not need to open folders or type commands.

START
  Double-click Floorline on the Desktop.
  Wait a few seconds. The app opens full screen.

STOP
  Click Exit at the top, or press Esc.
  Then double-click Stop Floorline on the Desktop.

FIRST TIME
  Click "Load demo production share", or choose XML files from the share.

FULL SCREEN
  Floorline opens full screen by itself.
  If you can see the Windows or Mac desktop behind it, click Full screen at the top.

ANOTHER PC
  Click Share at the top and send that person the address.
  They can use Windows, a Mac, or Linux.

If Floorline does not open, double-click the installer once (install-windows.cmd or install-macos.command) and try Floorline again.
